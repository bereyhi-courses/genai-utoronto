description: In this lecture, we learn the well-known case of DPMs, i.e., Denoising DPM (DDPM). We see how we build the denoiser in these models and how we can train and sample from them. This describes the most conventional form of diffusion models used in practice. We also study the Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models (DDIMs) which enable us to sample much faster as compared to DDPMs. Finally, we give some well-known examples for diffusion models that are currently being used in large scale.
